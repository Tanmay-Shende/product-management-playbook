# AI Product Strategy

## Purpose
Identify where AI creates enough customer or business value to justify investment.

## When to use
AI features, AI-native products, workflow automation, and agent opportunities.

## Procedure
1. Define the workflow and user goal.
2. Identify where intelligence adds value.
3. Separate deterministic from probabilistic steps.
4. Define human oversight.
5. Define evaluation and failure handling.
6. Estimate economics.
7. Run a bounded experiment.
8. Define scale criteria.

## Output
AI opportunity, workflow, AI role, data/model needs, evaluation, controls, economics, experiment, and scale criteria.

## Guardrails
Do not use AI simply because it is available. Explain why it changes the outcome.
