# Build, Buy, or Partner

## Purpose
Choose the best sourcing model for a needed capability.

## When to use
When a capability could be built internally, acquired, licensed, or sourced from a partner.

## Procedure
1. Define the capability.
2. Assess strategic differentiation.
3. Estimate time, cost, and operating burden.
4. Assess partner maturity and switching risk.
5. Evaluate control, data, and dependency implications.
6. Compare options.
7. Define transition or exit conditions.

## Output
Capability definition, option comparison, recommendation, dependencies, and exit strategy.
