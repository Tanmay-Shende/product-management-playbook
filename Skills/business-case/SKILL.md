# Business Case

## Purpose
Evaluate whether an investment is worth making.

## When to use
New products, major features, market entry, platform investment, or funding requests.

## Procedure
1. Define the decision.
2. Quantify the opportunity.
3. Compare alternatives, including doing nothing.
4. Estimate incremental benefits and costs.
5. Model base, upside, and downside cases.
6. Surface risks and assumptions.
7. Make a clear recommendation.

## Output
Decision requested, opportunity, options, economics, risks, assumptions, and recommendation.

## Guardrails
Do not invent material facts. Separate facts, assumptions, estimates, and recommendations.
