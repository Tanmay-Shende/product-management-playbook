# Competitive Analysis

## Purpose
Understand alternatives, differentiation, competitive threats, and whitespace.

## When to use
Strategy, positioning, market entry, pricing, and investment decisions.

## Procedure
1. Define the customer decision being won.
2. Identify direct, indirect, internal, and substitute alternatives.
3. Compare outcomes, not just features.
4. Separate table stakes from differentiators.
5. Identify structural advantages and weaknesses.
6. Assess likely competitive response.
7. Translate findings into strategic choices.

## Output
Landscape, buying criteria, table stakes, differentiators, threats, whitespace, and implications.

## Guardrails
Use evidence. Do not infer competitor capabilities without support.
