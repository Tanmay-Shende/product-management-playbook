# Customer Discovery

## Purpose
Learn what customers do, need, value, and will change.

## When to use
Before committing to a product direction or when assumptions are uncertain.

## Procedure
1. Define the learning objective.
2. Form explicit hypotheses.
3. Choose representative customers.
4. Ask neutral questions about current behavior.
5. Capture evidence and exceptions.
6. Separate stated preference from observed behavior.
7. Update hypotheses and decide what to test next.

## Output
Research objective, questions, evidence, themes, contradictions, updated hypotheses, and product implications.

## Guardrails
Do not treat a single customer's opinion as market truth.
