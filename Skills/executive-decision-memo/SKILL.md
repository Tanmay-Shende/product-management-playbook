# Executive Decision Memo

## Purpose
Help senior leaders make a clear decision quickly.

## When to use
Major strategy, investment, product, organization, or risk decisions.

## Procedure
1. State the decision requested.
2. Lead with the recommendation.
3. Explain why now.
4. Present essential evidence.
5. Show options and tradeoffs.
6. Explain economics and risks.
7. State what would change the recommendation.
8. Specify the next action.

## Output
Executive summary, decision, context, options, recommendation, economics, risks, execution implications, and next action.
