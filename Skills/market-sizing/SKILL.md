# Market Sizing

## Purpose
Estimate the economic opportunity using transparent assumptions.

## When to use
Strategy, market entry, business cases, and portfolio decisions.

## Procedure
1. Define the market boundary.
2. Choose top-down, bottom-up, or both.
3. Build from observable drivers.
4. Label facts versus assumptions.
5. Create scenarios.
6. Stress-test major assumptions.
7. State confidence and limitations.

## Output
Market definition, TAM/SAM/SOM or equivalent, calculation, assumptions, scenarios, and confidence.

## Guardrails
Never present an estimate as a fact. Make major assumptions visible.
