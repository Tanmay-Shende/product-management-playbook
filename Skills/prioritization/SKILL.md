# Product Prioritization

## Purpose
Allocate finite people, capital, and time across competing opportunities.

## When to use
Roadmap, quarterly planning, portfolio, or investment decisions.

## Procedure
1. Define the portfolio objective.
2. Separate mandatory work from discretionary bets.
3. Choose decision criteria.
4. Assess customer impact, business value, strategic leverage, risk, effort, and dependencies.
5. Compare scenarios.
6. Make the allocation decision.

## Output
Prioritization principles, ranked or grouped opportunities, allocation, deferred work, and rationale.

## Guardrails
A scoring model should structure judgment, not replace it.
