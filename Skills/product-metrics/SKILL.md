# Product Metrics

## Purpose
Choose a small set of metrics that explain customer and business outcomes.

## When to use
Product health, experimentation, executive reviews, and operating cadence.

## Procedure
1. Define the product outcome.
2. Map the key customer behavior.
3. Identify leading and lagging indicators.
4. Balance customer, business, quality, and risk metrics.
5. Define formulas and owners.
6. Set targets and review cadence.

## Output
Primary outcome, supporting metrics, definitions, targets, owners, and review cadence.
