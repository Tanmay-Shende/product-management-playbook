# Product Requirements

## Purpose
Translate customer and business needs into precise, testable requirements.

## When to use
After the problem and desired outcome are sufficiently understood.

## Procedure
1. Define the outcome.
2. Identify users and use cases.
3. Write functional and non-functional requirements.
4. Define acceptance criteria.
5. Capture edge cases and dependencies.
6. State what is out of scope.
7. Define success metrics.

## Output
Objective, use cases, requirements, acceptance criteria, constraints, dependencies, metrics, and out of scope.
