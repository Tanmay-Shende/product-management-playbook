# Product Roadmap

## Purpose
Communicate how product investment will create measurable outcomes over time.

## When to use
Planning, executive alignment, customer communication, and operating cadence.

## Procedure
1. Start from outcomes.
2. Group work into themes or strategic bets.
3. Sequence by value, dependency, and learning.
4. Separate commitments from exploration.
5. Show meaningful milestones.
6. Tie work to measurable outcomes.
7. Revisit based on evidence.

## Output
Outcome-based roadmap with themes, initiatives, timing, dependencies, and success measures.
