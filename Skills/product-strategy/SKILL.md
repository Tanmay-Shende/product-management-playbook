# Product Strategy

## Purpose
Define where to play, what problem to solve, how to differentiate, and how to create durable value.

## When to use
Product direction, annual planning, market entry, or strategy resets.

## Procedure
1. Define the target customer and problem.
2. Explain why the problem matters now.
3. Identify strategic choices and non-choices.
4. Define differentiated value.
5. Identify required capabilities.
6. Connect customer value to business value.
7. Set measurable outcomes.

## Output
Strategy statement, target customer, problem, strategic choices, differentiation, capabilities, business model, and metrics.
