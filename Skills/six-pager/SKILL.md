# Six-Pager

## Purpose
Develop a rigorous narrative for a complex product or business decision.

## When to use
Executive strategy, investment proposals, operating-model changes, or major product narratives.

## Procedure
1. State the thesis or decision.
2. Explain what changed and why it matters.
3. Present the strategic model and evidence.
4. Describe customer and business implications.
5. Explain competitive advantage and tradeoffs.
6. Close with recommendations, decisions, and open questions.

## Output
A self-contained narrative that can stand alone without a live presentation.

## Guardrails
Prefer clear argument and evidence over volume of content.
