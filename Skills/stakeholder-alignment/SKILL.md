# Stakeholder Alignment

## Purpose
Create shared understanding, clarify disagreement, and establish decision ownership.

## When to use
When product, engineering, commercial, risk, finance, operations, or leadership stakeholders have different priorities or interpretations.

## Procedure
1. Map stakeholders and decision rights.
2. Understand interests and constraints.
3. Separate disagreement on facts from disagreement on choices.
4. Establish shared facts.
5. Make tradeoffs explicit.
6. Resolve or escalate the decision.
7. Document the decision and follow-up.

## Output
Stakeholder map, shared facts, disagreements, decision rights, decision, and actions.
